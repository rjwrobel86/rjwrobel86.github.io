Where it fits

Three rows without moving all the way to a full-size truck-based SUV

The 2027 Traverse is Chevrolet's spacious unibody three-row SUV. It is designed around family hauling, road trips, school schedules, sports equipment, and the recurring mystery of how one household acquired so many bags.

Available seating for up to eight and up to 98 cubic feet of maximum cargo volume place Traverse well above compact SUVs for flexibility. Unlike Tahoe and Suburban, Traverse uses a turbocharged four-cylinder powertrain and a car-like unibody structure.

Best fit for

  • Families that need a genuinely useful third row and substantial cargo volume.
  • Drivers who want three-row space without moving to the larger Tahoe or Suburban platform.
  • Road-trip households that value a large infotainment interface and available advanced driver assistance.

Consider another Chevy when

  • Choose Equinox when five seats are enough and lower cost and easier maneuvering matter.
  • Choose Tahoe for higher available towing, V8 or diesel power, and full-size SUV capability.
  • Choose Suburban for maximum passenger-plus-cargo room behind all three rows.

Trim selection is intentionally focused. LT covers broad family needs, Z71 adds off-road-oriented capability and styling, and RS emphasizes technology, appearance, and a more premium cabin.

Interior, utility, and technology

A family cabin built around people, screens, bags, and actual life

The standard 17.7-inch center display is one of the Traverse's signature features. An 11-inch Driver Information Center provides a second large information surface directly ahead of the driver.

Up to eight-passenger seating is available, while captain's-chair configurations can improve access to the third row. The correct layout depends on whether maximum seat count or easier third-row entry matters more.

Maximum cargo volume reaches 98 cubic feet with rear rows folded. For realistic shopping, inspect space behind the third row with all seats occupied, especially if you travel with strollers, sports bags, or luggage.

Frequently asked questions

2027 Chevrolet Traverse FAQs

How many people does the 2027 Chevrolet Traverse seat?

Traverse offers seating for up to eight, depending on trim and second-row configuration.

How much cargo space does Traverse have?

Chevrolet lists up to 98 cubic feet of maximum cargo volume with the rear seating areas configured for cargo.

What engine is in the 2027 Traverse?

The 2027 Traverse uses a 2.5-liter turbocharged engine paired with an eight-speed automatic transmission.

Is all-wheel drive available on Traverse?

Yes. All-wheel drive is available, and Z71 is the most off-road-oriented configuration.

What is the difference between Traverse LT, Z71, and RS?

LT is the broad family-value trim. Z71 adds more rugged and off-road-oriented equipment. RS emphasizes sport styling, technology, and premium content.

Does Traverse have Super Cruise?

Super Cruise is available and is standard on RS with the applicable equipment and service plan. It works only under defined conditions and does not make the vehicle autonomous.

Is Traverse bigger than Equinox?

Yes. Traverse is a three-row midsize SUV with substantially greater passenger and cargo capacity.

Should I buy Traverse or Tahoe?

Traverse is generally the more efficient, lower-cost, car-like family choice. Tahoe is larger, uses a truck-based platform, offers V8 or diesel power, and can tow more when properly equipped.

Does the third row fit adults?

Traverse is designed as a true three-row SUV, but comfort depends on passenger height, seat position, trip length, and configuration. Adults who will use the third row should sit in it during the test drive.
