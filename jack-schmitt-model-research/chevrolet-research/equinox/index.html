Where it fits

The middle of the Chevy SUV lineup, in a good way

The Equinox gives you noticeably more room than Chevrolet's smallest SUVs without committing to the size, third row, or price of a Traverse, Tahoe, or Suburban.

Its standard 1.5-liter turbocharged engine produces 175 horsepower and 203 lb-ft of torque. Available all-wheel drive adds confidence for wet pavement, winter weather, gravel, and changing road conditions.

Best fit for

  • Drivers who want more space than Trax or Trailblazer
  • Small families that do not need a third row
  • Buyers who want available all-wheel drive
  • Shoppers who value cargo flexibility and easy daily use

Consider another Chevy when

  • You need three-row seating
  • You tow more than 1,500 pounds
  • You want the lowest possible Chevrolet SUV price
  • You are ready to switch to an electric vehicle

Five-passenger seating and up to 63.5 cubic feet of cargo volume make the Equinox a practical choice for couples, small families, commuters, and drivers who want SUV versatility without an oversized footprint.

Turbocharged response

The 1.5-liter turbo is tuned for everyday acceleration, merging, commuting, and road-trip use rather than high-performance theatrics.

Available all-wheel drive

AWD can send power where traction is needed, making it useful for wet roads, winter weather, and loose surfaces.

Drive Mode Selector

Standard drive modes help adapt vehicle behavior to changing driving conditions.

Light trailering

Up to 1,500 pounds of towing can handle a small properly equipped trailer. Heavier jobs belong to a Colorado, Silverado, Tahoe, or Suburban.

Capability note: Maximum towing applies only when the vehicle is properly equipped. Passengers, cargo, accessories, hitch equipment, and trailer load all affect safe trailering capacity.

Interior, cargo, and technology

A modern cabin with space that earns its keep

The Equinox offers up to 63.5 cubic feet of maximum cargo volume, giving it room for luggage, sports equipment, groceries, home-project supplies, and the other cargo that never appears in polished SUV commercials.

An available AutoSense power liftgate can simplify loading, while concealed storage beneath the rear cargo floor helps keep smaller items organized and out of sight.

The personalized 11.3-inch infotainment touchscreen supports compatible Apple CarPlay and Android Auto features, OnStar connectivity, voice commands, and available Google built-in services.

Safety and driver assistance

More than 15 standard safety and driver-assistance features

Chevy Safety Assist includes six core technologies. These systems can support an attentive driver, but they do not replace careful driving or eliminate operating limits.

Automatic Emergency Braking

Can help reduce the severity of certain detected frontal collisions.

Front Pedestrian Braking

Can alert the driver and provide braking support in certain pedestrian situations.

Lane Keep Assist

Works with Lane Departure Warning to provide support in certain unintended lane departures.

Forward Collision Alert

Can warn when the system detects a potential front-end collision.

Following Distance Indicator

Displays estimated following time behind a detected vehicle ahead.

IntelliBeam

Can automatically switch between high and low beams under certain conditions.

Frequently asked questions

2027 Chevrolet Equinox FAQs

How many people does the 2027 Chevrolet Equinox seat?

The 2027 Equinox seats up to five people.

Is all-wheel drive available on the 2027 Equinox?

Yes. All-wheel drive is available. Check the exact vehicle listing or window label to confirm drivetrain equipment.

What engine does the 2027 Equinox use?

The 2027 Equinox uses a 1.5-liter turbocharged engine producing 175 horsepower and 203 lb-ft of torque.

How much cargo room does the Equinox have?

Chevrolet lists up to 63.5 cubic feet of maximum cargo volume when the rear seating area is configured for cargo.

How much can the 2027 Equinox tow?

When properly equipped, the 2027 Equinox can tow up to 1,500 pounds. Actual capacity depends on equipment, passengers, cargo, hitch hardware, and trailer load.

What is the difference between LT, RS, and ACTIV?

LT emphasizes value and everyday features. RS adds sportier blacked-out styling and larger wheel choices. ACTIV adds all-terrain tires, a uniquely tuned suspension, and rugged design details.

How long is the 2027 Chevrolet Equinox?

Chevrolet lists an overall length of 183.2 inches, which is slightly more than 15 feet.

How much does the 2027 Equinox weigh?

Chevrolet lists an unloaded weight range of approximately 3,428 to 3,629 pounds, depending on drivetrain, trim, and equipment.

Does the Equinox include Apple CarPlay and Android Auto?

The 11.3-inch infotainment system supports compatible Apple CarPlay and Android Auto features. Availability and functionality can depend on the device, plan, and vehicle equipment.
