Where it fits

An electric SUV designed to make the switch feel practical

The 2026 Equinox EV is Chevrolet's mainstream electric compact SUV. Front-wheel-drive models offer up to an EPA-estimated 319 miles of range, while all-wheel-drive models offer up to an EPA-estimated 307 miles.

EV ownership works best when charging is routine rather than an emergency. A home Level 2 charger can restore daily driving overnight, while public DC fast charging supports longer trips. Charging speed and time vary with charger power, battery temperature, state of charge, weather, and vehicle conditions.

Best fit for

  • Drivers who can charge at home or work and want to replace most gas-station visits with plugging in.
  • Commuters whose typical daily mileage is comfortably below the available range.
  • Buyers seeking a five-passenger EV with a large display and conventional SUV practicality.

Consider another Chevy when

  • Choose gasoline Equinox when charging access is poor or long-distance travel is unusually unpredictable.
  • Choose a plug-in or gasoline vehicle if you regularly tow, travel beyond reliable charging corridors, or cannot install charging.
  • Compare insurance, home-charging installation, electricity rates, incentives, and resale rather than focusing only on MSRP.

Equinox EV seats five and provides up to 57.2 cubic feet of cargo volume. It is not simply a gasoline Equinox with batteries added; it uses a dedicated electric design, different dimensions, and a different ownership pattern.

Interior, utility, and technology

A quiet, technology-forward cabin with everyday SUV usefulness

A 17.7-inch center display and 11-inch Driver Information Center give Equinox EV a technology-forward cabin with large, readable information surfaces.

Up to 57.2 cubic feet of maximum cargo volume supports everyday SUV use. The rear seats fold for larger loads, while five-passenger seating covers typical commuter and small-family needs.

Because EVs are quiet at low speeds and deliver immediate torque, the test drive should include city traffic, highway merging, regenerative braking, and parking rather than a five-minute loop around the block.

Frequently asked questions

2026 Chevrolet Equinox EV FAQs

What is the range of the 2026 Chevrolet Equinox EV?

Front-wheel-drive models offer up to an EPA-estimated 319 miles. All-wheel-drive models offer up to an EPA-estimated 307 miles. Actual range varies with weather, speed, terrain, climate use, load, battery condition, and driving style.

How long does Equinox EV take to charge?

Charging time depends on charger type, available power, battery temperature, and starting state of charge. A home Level 2 charger is generally the practical overnight solution, while DC fast charging is intended for quicker travel stops.

Can I charge Equinox EV at a Tesla Supercharger?

Chevrolet states that compatible vehicles can access participating Tesla Superchargers using approved equipment and app support. Station compatibility, adapters, accounts, and availability should be verified before travel.

Does Equinox EV come with all-wheel drive?

All-wheel drive is available on qualifying configurations.

How much cargo space does Equinox EV have?

Chevrolet lists up to 57.2 cubic feet of maximum cargo volume.

How many people does Equinox EV seat?

It seats up to five.

What is the difference between Equinox and Equinox EV?

Equinox uses a gasoline turbo engine and can be refueled quickly almost anywhere. Equinox EV uses battery-electric power, requires charging, and offers a different platform, range profile, and ownership cost structure.

Do I need a home charger?

It is not technically mandatory, but reliable home or workplace charging dramatically improves convenience. Renters and drivers without dedicated parking should map realistic public charging before buying.

Does cold weather reduce EV range?

Yes. Cold temperatures, cabin heating, battery conditioning, and winter road conditions can reduce range. Preconditioning while plugged in and planning a larger reserve can help.
