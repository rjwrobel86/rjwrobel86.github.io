Where it fits

The step up when light-duty numbers stop being enough

Silverado HD includes 2500 HD and 3500 HD configurations designed for work beyond light-duty pickup limits. The maximum available diesel towing rating reaches 36,000 pounds in the proper configuration.

Two 6.6-liter V8 engines are offered. The gasoline V8 produces 401 horsepower and 464 lb-ft of torque. The Duramax turbo-diesel produces 470 horsepower and 975 lb-ft of torque. Both pair with a 10-speed Allison automatic transmission.

Best fit for

  • Commercial, agricultural, construction, equipment, and recreational buyers with heavy trailer requirements.
  • Drivers who need fifth-wheel or gooseneck capability beyond Silverado 1500 limits.
  • Buyers who can justify the cost, size, ride, fuel use, and maintenance of a true heavy-duty truck.

Consider another Chevy when

  • Choose Silverado 1500 when your loaded trailer and payload remain safely within light-duty ratings.
  • Choose Colorado for smaller jobs, easier parking, and lower ownership cost.
  • Review commercial chassis-cab models when upfits, service bodies, or vocational equipment are required.

Heavy-duty buying requires precision. Trailer type, loaded weight, pin or tongue weight, axle ratings, payload, cab, bed, drivetrain, hitch, passengers, and cargo all matter. A maximum headline number never applies to every truck.

Frequently asked questions

2026 Chevrolet Silverado HD FAQs

How much can the 2026 Silverado HD tow?

The maximum available diesel rating is 36,000 pounds in the proper configuration. Gasoline configurations can reach up to 19,080 pounds. Exact ratings vary significantly.

What is the difference between Silverado 2500 HD and 3500 HD?

3500 HD supports the highest available payload and towing ratings and offers dual-rear-wheel configurations. 2500 HD may provide all the capacity many buyers need with somewhat easier daily use.

How much torque does the Duramax diesel make?

The available 6.6-liter Duramax turbo-diesel produces 975 lb-ft of torque.

What transmission does Silverado HD use?

Both the 6.6-liter gasoline V8 and Duramax diesel pair with a 10-speed Allison automatic transmission.

Should I buy the gas or diesel Silverado HD?

Gas can reduce purchase cost and complexity for lower annual mileage and moderate loads. Diesel provides much more torque and the highest tow ratings. Fuel cost, maintenance, duty cycle, resale, and trailer weight should all be evaluated.

What is the maximum gas-engine tow rating?

Chevrolet lists up to 19,080 pounds with the 6.6-liter gasoline V8 in the proper configuration.

Does Silverado HD offer a dually?

Yes. Silverado 3500 HD is available with dual rear wheels in qualifying configurations.

What is the difference between Silverado HD ZR2 and High Country?

ZR2 is the specialized off-road heavy-duty trim. High Country is the luxury flagship. Both are expensive, but they solve very different problems.

Can a Silverado HD be a daily driver?

Yes, but buyers should accept its size, turning radius, ride, parking demands, fuel use, and higher operating costs. It is most rational when its capability is regularly used.
