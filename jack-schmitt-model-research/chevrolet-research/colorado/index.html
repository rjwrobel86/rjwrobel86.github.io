Where it fits

A midsize truck with real towing and off-road depth

Colorado concentrates Chevrolet truck capability into a midsize footprint. Every trim uses the TurboMax 2.7-liter engine, rated at 310 horsepower and 430 lb-ft of torque, paired with an eight-speed automatic.

The lineup runs from WT and Custom through LT, Trail Boss, Z71, and ZR2. That creates a clean progression from work and value to comfort, trail use, and specialized off-road performance.

Best fit for

  • Drivers who need real pickup utility without the width and length of a full-size truck.
  • Buyers who tow boats, small campers, utility trailers, or recreation equipment within midsize limits.
  • Off-road shoppers comparing Trail Boss, Z71, and ZR2.

Consider another Chevy when

  • Choose Silverado 1500 for more cab, bed, payload, and maximum towing options.
  • Choose Silverado HD when commercial or large-trailer requirements exceed light-duty capability.
  • Choose an SUV when enclosed cargo and rear-seat comfort matter more than an open bed.

Maximum towing reaches 7,700 pounds when properly equipped. As with every truck, the exact rating depends on configuration, passengers, cargo, hitch setup, and the certification information for the individual vehicle.

Interior, utility, and technology

A modern crew-cab workspace without full-size bulk

Colorado uses a crew-cab format that supports regular passenger use while preserving a practical pickup bed. Rear-seat shoppers should still test knee room and seating position because midsize trucks are narrower than full-size models.

The standard 11.3-inch infotainment touchscreen and 11-inch Driver Information Center provide a consistent technology baseline across the lineup.

Bed utility, camera features, off-road displays, and power accessories vary by trim and package. Match the equipment to actual use rather than buying an off-road badge for a life spent entirely between asphalt parking lines.

Frequently asked questions

2026 Chevrolet Colorado FAQs

How much can the 2026 Chevrolet Colorado tow?

The maximum available rating is 7,700 pounds when properly equipped. The rating for a specific truck depends on configuration, equipment, passengers, cargo, and trailer setup.

What engine is in the 2026 Colorado?

Every 2026 Colorado trim uses the TurboMax 2.7-liter turbocharged engine rated at 310 horsepower and 430 lb-ft of torque.

Does Colorado come with a V6?

No. The current Colorado lineup uses the TurboMax turbocharged four-cylinder engine rather than a V6.

What is the difference between Colorado Trail Boss, Z71, and ZR2?

Trail Boss is the more accessible lifted off-road trim. Z71 combines trail capability with more on-road comfort and equipment. ZR2 is the most specialized factory off-road configuration.

Is Colorado available with four-wheel drive?

Yes. Four-wheel drive is available and standard on several off-road-focused trims.

How big is the Colorado touchscreen?

An 11.3-inch infotainment touchscreen is standard.

Should I buy Colorado or Silverado 1500?

Choose Colorado for easier maneuvering and midsize utility. Choose Silverado 1500 for greater cabin, bed, powertrain, payload, and towing choices.

Can Colorado tow a camper?

It can tow many smaller campers when the loaded trailer weight, tongue weight, passengers, cargo, hitch, and truck configuration remain within all ratings. Verify actual loaded weight rather than brochure dry weight.

How many people does Colorado seat?

Colorado uses a crew-cab layout and seats up to five.
