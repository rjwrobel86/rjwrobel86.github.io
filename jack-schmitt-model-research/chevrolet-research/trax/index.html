Where it fits

The easiest entry point into a new Chevrolet SUV

The Trax is Chevrolet's lowest-priced SUV and one of the clearest value propositions in the lineup. It seats five, offers up to 54 cubic feet of cargo space, and provides a larger available infotainment display than shoppers may expect at its price.

The key limitation is simple: Trax is front-wheel drive only. That is completely reasonable for many St. Louis-area commuters, but shoppers who specifically want all-wheel drive should compare Trailblazer and Equinox before deciding.

Best fit for

  • Budget-conscious shoppers who want SUV styling, a comfortable seating position, and modern technology.
  • Commuters and first-time new-vehicle buyers who do not require all-wheel drive or heavy towing.
  • Drivers who need more cargo flexibility than a small sedan without moving into a larger compact SUV.

Consider another Chevy when

  • Choose Trailblazer if available all-wheel drive or a shorter overall vehicle is important.
  • Choose Equinox for more cargo room, available all-wheel drive, and greater towing capability.
  • Choose a used larger SUV if maximum space matters more than new-vehicle warranty and fuel economy.

Its 1.2-liter turbocharged three-cylinder engine is tuned for daily transportation rather than towing or high-performance driving. The payoff is an EPA-estimated 30 mpg combined and a low entry price.

Interior, utility, and technology

More passenger and cargo usefulness than the price suggests

Up to 54 cubic feet of maximum cargo volume gives Trax real flexibility for luggage, shopping, small furniture, climbing gear, and other weekend equipment.

The available 11-inch infotainment display modernizes the cabin and provides room for compatible phone, media, and vehicle functions.

Trax seats five, but its ideal use is often one to four people with flexible cargo needs. Bring regular passengers to the test drive and check rear-seat comfort rather than relying on a specification sheet.

Frequently asked questions

2026 Chevrolet Trax FAQs

Does the 2026 Chevrolet Trax have all-wheel drive?

No. The 2026 Trax is front-wheel drive only. Trailblazer and Equinox are better Chevrolet comparisons when all-wheel drive is required.

How many people does the Trax seat?

Trax seats up to five people.

How much cargo space does the Trax have?

Chevrolet lists up to 54 cubic feet of maximum cargo volume.

What is the 2026 Trax fuel economy?

Chevrolet lists an EPA-estimated 30 mpg combined. Actual mileage varies with speed, weather, traffic, maintenance, load, and driving style.

What engine does the Trax use?

The Trax uses an ECOTEC 1.2-liter turbocharged three-cylinder engine paired with a six-speed automatic transmission.

Can a Chevrolet Trax tow a trailer?

Trax is not positioned as a tow vehicle and Chevrolet does not promote a conventional maximum tow rating for it. Buyers with towing needs should compare Trailblazer, Equinox, or a Chevrolet truck.

Which Trax trim is the best value?

LS has the lowest price. LT is often the strongest all-around value because it adds desirable technology and convenience while remaining close to the entry price. The right choice depends on current inventory and packages.

What is the difference between Trax 2RS and ACTIV?

Both sit at the top of the lineup. 2RS emphasizes sporty styling, while ACTIV uses more rugged-inspired exterior and interior details.

Is Trax a good first new vehicle?

It can be. Trax combines a low starting price, five-passenger seating, useful cargo volume, modern safety content, and new-vehicle warranty coverage. Insurance and total ownership cost should still be compared.
