Where it fits

Small-SUV dimensions with an available all-wheel-drive escape hatch

Trailblazer is Chevrolet's smaller all-wheel-drive-capable SUV. It is compact enough for dense parking lots and city streets, but its fold-flat front passenger seat creates room for cargo up to 8.5 feet long.

Two turbocharged engines are used across the lineup. The available 1.3-liter engine produces up to 174 lb-ft of torque and supports available all-wheel drive configurations.

Best fit for

  • Drivers who want a small SUV with available all-wheel drive.
  • Urban and suburban shoppers who value easy parking and flexible cargo arrangements.
  • Buyers comparing Trax value with Equinox space and looking for a middle path.

Consider another Chevy when

  • Choose Trax when lowest price and maximum value matter more than all-wheel drive.
  • Choose Equinox when rear-seat and cargo space are higher priorities.
  • Choose Traverse if you need a third row or regularly carry more than five people.

Trailblazer's value is not raw size. It is adaptability: five seats, available traction, a standard 11-inch touchscreen, and a footprint that remains easy to live with.

Interior, utility, and technology

Compact outside, flexible enough inside to avoid feeling toy-sized

The fold-flat front passenger seat is unusually useful. It allows long objects, including certain flat-pack furniture, lumber, outdoor equipment, and other awkward cargo, to extend farther through the cabin.

The standard 11-inch touchscreen gives every trim a modern interface rather than reserving the largest display for the top of the lineup.

Five-passenger seating is best evaluated in person. Trailblazer is a small SUV, so regular adult rear passengers should test legroom and seat comfort.

Frequently asked questions

2026 Chevrolet Trailblazer FAQs

Does the 2026 Chevrolet Trailblazer offer all-wheel drive?

Yes. All-wheel drive is available on qualifying configurations.

How much can Trailblazer tow?

When properly equipped, Trailblazer can tow up to 1,000 pounds.

What engines are available in Trailblazer?

The lineup uses turbocharged 1.2-liter and available 1.3-liter engines. The 1.3-liter produces up to 174 lb-ft of torque.

How many people does Trailblazer seat?

Trailblazer seats up to five.

Does Trailblazer have a large touchscreen?

Yes. An 11-inch infotainment touchscreen is standard.

What is the difference between Trax and Trailblazer?

Trax starts lower and offers more maximum cargo volume, while Trailblazer is smaller in overall concept and offers available all-wheel drive and up to 1,000 pounds of towing.

What are the ACTIV and RS trims?

ACTIV uses rugged styling and adventure-oriented tuning. RS uses sportier street-focused design details. Both sit above LS and LT.

Can Trailblazer carry long items?

The fold-flat front passenger seat allows items up to approximately 8.5 feet long to extend through the cabin when properly positioned.

Is Trailblazer good in snow?

Available all-wheel drive can improve traction, but tires, speed, road conditions, and driver decisions remain critical. All-wheel drive does not shorten stopping distance on ice.
